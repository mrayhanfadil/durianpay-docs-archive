# Preparing your fund

Before you can process payouts, you will need to allocate funds into your Durianpay account. There are three methods to top up your account:

1. Payment via Durianpay which is settled to your Durianpay balance
2. Unique Amount Bank Transfer
3. Virtual Account/ VA Static Transfer

For more details you can refer to [Preparing your fund](https://docs.durianpay.id/docs/preparing-your-fund)
