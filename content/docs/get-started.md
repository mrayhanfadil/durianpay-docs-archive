# Integration Overview

## Get Your Account

1. Contact our team (<sales@durianpay.id>) to start creating your account. If you're eligible, a dedicated account manager will be assigned to support you every step of the way.
2. Your account manager will require ask for some basic informations to setup your merchant account.
3. Verify your email. Then, you can start accessing your account on [dashboard.](https://dashboard.durianpay.id)

## Start Integrating

1. Choose the [Integration type](https://docs.durianpay.id/docs/overvew) that suits your needs.
2. Start integrating your website or app with our APIs, SDKs and Plugins in the Sandbox Mode.
3. Test your integration by creating test transactions. Please refer here to [test your integrations.](https://docs.durianpay.id/docs/simulating-test-payments)

## Start Accepting Real Transactions

1. Submit the required documents to enable your account in LIVE mode. Refer to the [basic documents list](https://durianpay.readme.io/docs/uat-form)  here to start accepting real transactions.
2. After receiving your complete set of documents, our team will start the verification process. This may involve phone or video verification and additional checks if needed (**estimated time**: 7 working days).
3. Submit your integration test result (UAT) to <integrations@durianpay.id.> You will be provided a UAT document by your account manager to complete.
4. After your documents are verified and your UAT results has met all requirements, you’ll receive LIVE mode credentials to start accepting transactions in the LIVE environment.
