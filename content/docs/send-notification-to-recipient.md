# Send notification to recipient

Durianpay provides 2 means of notification to pay out recipient: Email and Whatsapp. When a pay out is successfully paid  to the end recipient and email/ phone number is inserted in the details of the payout, then Durianpay will send the notification of success disbursement to recipient email/ Whatsapp.

To Setup the notification you can access menu `Settings` and click sub-menu of Notification as following. The settings will be shown in Disbursement Consumer Notification.
