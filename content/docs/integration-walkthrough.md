# Overview

Accept payments in your website, app OR get started with NO CODE integration

Durianpay empowers businesses with a streamlined payment solution tailored for Southeast Asia, merging diverse payment methods and providers into one easy-to-use platform. Through a single integration, merchants can access a complete payment network, ensuring every transaction is seamless and reliable.

## Why use Durianpay for accepting payments?

![](https://files.readme.io/3d8d74352c98168aacf5fcf18f2ba9683b933a2b42ec78dc62bd8e935ffb6a7d-image.png)

Think of Durianpay as a Merchant payment system integrator. In the back end, Durianpay connects to multiple PGs, Banks operators, e-wallets, and BNPL providers to ensure payment rail completion and redundancy. Through our routing capability, Durianpay makes sure each transaction goes through and reduces drop-off.

If there is/are additional payment methods merchants desire to add, don’t need to sweat because Durianpay got it covered for merchants through one-time integration. This also means that merchants no longer need to maintain multiple dashboards and deal with multiple settlements SLAs and dispute resolutions.

<br />

## Key Features & Benefits

1. Seamless payment processing: Integrate payment gateways for credit cards, e-wallets, virtual accounts, and local payment methods.
2. Fast, secure, and reliable transactions: Enjoy real-time transactions backed by ISO27001-certified protection and battle-tested reliability.
3. Dynamic routing: Optimizes transaction stability by automatically directing traffic to the most reliable provider, enhancing the consistency of payment services.
