# Use Cases

Durianpay’s sub-account feature is applicable for various use cases:

* **Merchant aggregator** - Acquire profits from the marked-up transaction fee
* **SaaS provider** - Collect platform fee and distribute payment to your customers
* **Businesses with multiple branch (franchise, retailers)** - Manage flow of funds and accept payments on behalf of your branch
* **Online marketplace, crowdsourcing platform** - Acquire commission fee from the orders you facilitate for resellers, freelancers, etc
* Many more..
