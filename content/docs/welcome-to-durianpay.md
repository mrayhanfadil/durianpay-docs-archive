# Welcome to Durianpay

👋 Fast, secure, and scalable payments solution for accepting and sending payments across Indonesia

# Introduction

Durianpay is a next-generation **B2B payment stack** to receive and send money anywhere in Indonesia with a mission to modernize and democratize payments for businesses. Our robust APIs, SDKs, and suite of product features are designed to help you easily integrate payment solutions into your applications, enabling faster growth and broader market reach.

Durianpay provides diverse payment options, including credit cards, bank transfers, e-wallets, and regional methods designed for Southeast Asia. Access our full list of supported payment methods. With Durianpay, you can also transfer money to over 130 banks and e-wallets throughout Indonesia. View our comprehensive list of [supported pay out destinations](https://durianpay.readme.io/docs/supported-banks-limits) .

# Key Features

* **Seamless payment processing**: Integrate payment gateways for credit cards, e-wallets, virtual accounts, and local payment methods.
* **Dynamic routing**: Optimizes transaction stability by automatically directing traffic to the most reliable provider, enhancing the consistency of payment services.
* **Real-time pay outs**: Enables instant pay outs, available 24/7 to meet time-sensitive payment needs.
* **High-value disbursement capability**: Facilitates pay outs beyond standard bank limits, supporting large-scale transactions with ease.
* **Fast, secure, and reliable transactions**: Enjoy real-time transactions backed by ISO27001-certified protection and battle-tested reliability.<br />
