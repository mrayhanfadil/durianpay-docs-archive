# Required Documents

The following are the support documents which you have to submit before you can start accepting real transactions.

| Required Documents                                                                                                                                                                                          | Description                                                                                                          |
| :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Registration Form](https://view.officeapps.live.com/op/view.aspx?src=https%3A%2F%2Fstatic.durianpay.id%2F%255BGeneral%255D%2BDurianpay%2BRegistration%2BForm%2B-%2BPT%2Bor%2BCV.xlsx\&wdOrigin=BROWSELINK) | A document which covers the details of your business along with what products are you planning to use from Durianpay |
| Kartu Tanda Penduduk (KTP) / ID Card                                                                                                                                                                        | Owner's Indonesian National ID Card                                                                                  |
| Kartu Nomor Pokok Wajib Pajak (NPWP) / Tax Identity Number of the Owner                                                                                                                                     | Indonesian Tax Identification Number issued to individuals and businesses for tax purposes                           |
| Copy of Bank Account Book                                                                                                                                                                                   | First page of merchant's bank account book                                                                           |
| Business Logo                                                                                                                                                                                               | Logo of your business (PNG is preferred, max size 1MB)                                                               |

Additional documents may be required based on the features you plan to use or your line of business. For more information, please refer to Registration Form or consult your account manager.
