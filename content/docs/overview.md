# Overview

Disburse with Ease—130+ Destination, Zero Hassle & Real-time

## Why use Durianpay Pay Outs?

Durianpay Pay Outs is designed to streamline and enhance your disbursement process with robust features so you can manage payouts effortlessly with confidence and ease.

* High coverage of pay out destination: we supports more than 130 Banks and E-wallets so you can easily send out money to your desired recipient without hassle
* Real-time pay outs: Enables instant pay outs, available 24/7 to meet time-sensitive payment needs.
* High-value pay outs capability: Facilitates pay outs beyond standard bank limits, supporting large-scale transactions with ease.
* Support notification (Email / Whatsapp) to your recipient you send out money & receipt information.
* Super friendly and ready to use No-Code solution or Highly Customizable Pay Outs API according to your needs.

## Use Cases

Durianpay's disbursement feature is applicable to various use cases, here are some of the use cases which may fit your business:

* **Payroll and commissions/bonuses** - As company or business owner, you can use our batch to disburse salary, and commissions/bonuses to staff and agent partners easily.
* **E-Wallet tops up** - Paying unbanked workers is now become easy with our ability to disburse money into e-wallet account balance.
* **Loan transfer** - If you are fintech company that want to scale the business by automating loan transfer, you can use us to validated bank accounts first then disburse the loan.
* **Refund** - Whenever your customer asking for refund, we are ready to transfer the money back to them through multiple banks and e-wallets.
* **VA Transfer** - A Virtual Account (VA) is a unique identifier provided by banks, typically used for billing, e-commerce payments, and loan re-payment. With our service, you can effortlessly transfer funds to  banks VA.
