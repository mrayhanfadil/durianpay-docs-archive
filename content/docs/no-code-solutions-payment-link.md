# No Code Solutions - Payment Link

Generate a Payment Link in seconds through the Durianpay Dashboard and share it seamlessly via social media, email, WhatsApp, or directly on your website, without the need of integrating to your website. Perfect for businesses of all sizes, especially those without dedicated tech resources.

The Payment Link directs your customers to a secure Durianpay payment page, where they can use their preferred payment method. This page is optimized to function smoothly on all devices and can be tailored with your brand’s name and logo for a personalized experience.

## Key Features

* **Create, Share, and Collect—All in Minutes**: Generate a payment link in seconds through the Durianpay Dashboard, share it anywhere—via email, WhatsApp, social media, or your website—and start collecting payments instantly!
* **Automatic Notifications:**&#x49;nstant notifications ensure your customers stay informed with a quick WhatsApp message or email whenever a payment link is created.
* **Make It Yours**: Customize the payment page with your colors and logo to reflect your brand’s identity. Go to this page to see how: [Custom Checkout Experience](https://docs.durianpay.id/docs/custom-checkout-flow).
* **Diverse Payment Methods**: You can customize the available payment methods to align with your business needs and customer preferences.

If API integration is what you need, Payment Links can seamlessly fit into your systems.
